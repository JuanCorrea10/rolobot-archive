The Copper Screen

Outside the headquarters of a famous American spy agency stands a tall copper screen, punched through with hundreds of letters.

Four encrypted messages hide in it. Three have been read. The fourth has kept its secret since 1990, and it is one of the most famous unsolved codes in the world.

Wikipedia knows the sculpture. The surname of the man who made it is the key.
