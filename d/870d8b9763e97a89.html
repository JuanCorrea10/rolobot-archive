The Song of 2012

In January 2012, something began to sing on an anonymous imageboard.

It said it was looking for "highly intelligent individuals". It left puzzles across the internet and across the real world, in cities on several continents. It sang for three years. Then it went silent. Nobody has ever proven who it was.

Wikipedia remembers it under the name of an insect and four digits.

The insect is your key.
