Ten doors. Each one wants a key.

A key is a word, sometimes a number, and it is always hiding somewhere: on the page, under the page, or out in the world.

Nothing here is random. Everything is on purpose. Even the mistakes.

how does this work?

Type a key into the box behind each door. Capital letters, spaces and punctuation don't matter. Google is allowed. Wikipedia is allowed. Reading the source is allowed. Giving up is not.

The box understands a few other words, too. You'll find them.

Your progress is remembered in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. There is no server. There is only you and the doors.

The first door has no key. It has no handle either. It is simply very, very hard to see.
